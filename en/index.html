FAQ

The questions we are asked most often.

What is Jelakelly?

A green apple flavored liquid magnesium stick. One stick a day, taken straight from the sachet with no water. It is a general food (liquid tea), not a supplement tablet.

What is in it?

100 mg of magnesium gluconate from the U.S. and vitamins B1 and B6 from Germany. Instead of artificial sweeteners, it is flavored with Korean apple concentrate.

How do I take it?

Tear the top of the stick and squeeze it straight into your mouth. No water needed. One stick a day.

When should I take it?

There is no fixed time. Taking it at the same time each day makes it easier to keep going.

How many sticks are in a box?

15 sticks. 225 g in total (15 g × 15 sticks) — a 15-day supply at one a day.

Where can I buy it?

Naver Smart Store, Olive Young Online, and Olive Young Better at Gwanghwamun, Gangnam and Starfield Anseong.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes, through Naver Gift. A phone number is all you need — no shipping address.

Where is it made?

In a HACCP-certified facility in Korea. Distributed by Wonbaek Co., Ltd.
