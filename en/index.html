FAQ

Frequently asked

What is Jelakelly?

A green apple liquid magnesium stick. Tear the top and take it straight from the stick. It is a general food, not a health functional food.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes, through Naver Gift. A phone number is all you need — no shipping address.

Who is it a good gift for?

Parents, your closest friend, or anyone you are grateful to. The two sisters behind Jelakelly gave it to their parents first, then to their closest friends.

What does a gift cost?

One box (15 sticks) is KRW 17,900 and two boxes (30 sticks) are KRW 33,800.

How much magnesium is in one stick?

100 mg of magnesium per 15 g stick—32% of the Korean daily reference value (315 mg).

What is magnesium gluconate?

Magnesium bound to gluconic acid, an organic acid. Jelakelly uses magnesium gluconate from the U.S.

What is in it?

100 mg of magnesium gluconate from the U.S. and vitamins B1 and B6 from Germany, flavored with Korean apple concentrate and French xylitol.

How do I take it?

Tear the top and squeeze it straight into your mouth. No water needed, though a sip afterward is fine. One to three sticks a day.

When should I take it?

There is no fixed time. Taking it at the same time each day makes it easier to keep going.

How many sticks are in a box?

15 sticks, 225 g in total (15 g × 15 sticks).

Where can I buy it?

Naver Smart Store, Olive Young Online, and Olive Young Better at Gwanghwamun, Gangnam, COEX and Starfield Anseong.
